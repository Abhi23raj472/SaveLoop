FAQ

Good questions.

Do I need an account?

No. Paste a link and download. There is nothing to sign up for.

No. SaveLoop doesn't store media and doesn't log links or IP addresses.

No. SaveLoop only works with public posts.

Yes. It runs in any modern mobile browser, with nothing to install.
